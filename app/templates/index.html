Uganda Secondary School Report Card Management System

A school-based system where teachers enter marks and the school administrator can monitor entries from a phone, while report cards are generated online.

School Login

How the system works

1. Administrator

Creates teacher accounts and monitors marks entered by teachers.

2. Teacher

Signs in and records formative and summative marks.

3. Live database

Entries are saved to the school's PostgreSQL database so the administrator can see updates online.

4. Subscription

School access is controlled by a UGX 150,000 term subscription.
